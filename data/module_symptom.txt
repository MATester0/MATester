1   Perception
1.1     snapshot is wrong or incomplete compared with the environment (Wrong-S)
1.1.1   snapshot and environment have the same modality, but different content (SM)
1.1.2   snapshot and environment have different modalities, but different content (DM)
1.2     two environments are nearly the same, but their snapshots are different (Inconsistent-S)
1.2.1   snapshots and environments have the same modality, but different content (SM)
1.2.2   snapshots and environments have different modalities, but different content (DM)
1.3     snapshot has wrong format (Malformed-S)
1.4   	snapshot does not exist (Missing-S)
1.5/1.1.3   snapshot is labeled, but labels are wrong or incomplete (Mislabeled-S)
2   Planner
2.1     plan is internally contradictory (Conflict-P)
2.2     plan does not satisfy the constraints given by the snapshot (Inapplicable-P)
2.2.1   plan and snapshot have the same modality, but plan does not meet the requirements (SM)
2.2.2   plan and snapshot have different modalities, but plan does not meet the requirements (DM)
2.3     plan is not related to the task (Irrelevant-P)
2.3.1   task is over, but plan decides differently (Wrong-Stop)
2.3.2   task is not over, but plan decides differently (Wrong-Stop)
2.3.3   plan cannot promote the task (Wrong-Step)
2.4     plan has wrong format (Malformed-P)
2.5     tasks, snapshots and prompts are the same, but their plans are different and finally result in different execution results (Inconsistent-P)
2.5.1   plans and snapshots have the same modality, but plans are different (SM)
2.5.2   plans and snapshots have different modalities, but plans are different (DM)
2.6     models are not loaded correctly or plan does not exist (Missing-P)
2.7		prompts are not generated correctly (Wrong-PR)
3   Executor
3.1     action is different from the plan (Wrong-A)
3.2     action is not applicable to the environment (Inapplicable-A)
3.2.1   plan is wrong but the corresponding action is directly generated without extra checking (Wrong-P&A)
3.2.2   API called by the action is implemented wrong (Wrong-API)
3.3     reflection of the action's execution results is wrong (Wrong-R)
3.4   	action does not exist (Missing-A)
